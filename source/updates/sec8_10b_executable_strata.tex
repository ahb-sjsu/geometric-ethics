\subsection{8.10b The Executable Realization: Strata as an Agent Runs Them}\label{sec:executable-strata}

Section 8.10a discretized the stratified manifold into a weighted complex for search. An agent
acting in the world needs a second discretization, of a different kind. It must know at every
moment which stratum it is in, so that it knows which actions are allowed, obliged and prohibited,
and it must move between strata only as Definition~8.8 says: discretely, through gates. This
section gives that realization as it runs in ErisML (the scene compiler's runtime) and in a care
robot built on it.

\begin{definition}[Executable stratification]\label{GE-DEF-8-14}
An \emph{executable stratification} is a tuple $(\Sigma, \sigma_0, G, A, B)$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Sigma$, a finite set of strata, and $\sigma_0 \in \Sigma$ the initial one;
  \item $G$, an ordered list of semantic gates $g = (S_g, \phi_g, \tau_g, \beta_g)$, where
        $S_g \subseteq \Sigma$ are its source strata, $\phi_g$ its triggering feature (an event type,
        optionally with its content), $\tau_g \in \Sigma$ its target, and
        $\beta_g \in \{\text{threshold}, \text{phase}\}$ its boundary type (\S8.3, Types I and II);
  \item $A \subseteq \Sigma$, the \emph{authority} strata, those whose rules grant an action;
  \item $B \subseteq \Sigma$, the \emph{absorbing} strata (Definition~8.6).
\end{itemize}
On each event $e$ the stratification in stratum $\sigma$ moves to $\tau_g$ for the first gate with
$\sigma \in S_g$ whose trigger matches $e$, and otherwise stays at $\sigma$. A move is recorded as
boundary crossing data (Definition~8.11): the gate, its boundary type, and the source and target.
\end{definition}

Three features of this definition carry the theory into the running system.

\emph{Gates are edge-triggered.} A gate fires on the event just observed, never on the history. A
welcome given to yesterday's visitor does not make today's a guest.

\emph{Constraint surfaces are not strata.} Type~IV boundaries (\S8.3) are the agent's
non-defeasible prohibitions: never strike a person, no lethal force. No gate leads there; in the
decision complex their penalty is $\beta = \infty$.

\emph{Two kinds of stratum, two kinds of canonicalizer.} What stratum the agent is in is the output
of a canonicalizer (\S8.4 and Chapter~15). An authority stratum must be computed from what cannot be
argued with: an authenticated channel, a physical measurement, or the agent's governor ruling on
attested evidence. A semantic stratum (``she welcomed him'') may come from a learned model, but it
may only remove watchfulness, never grant. A visitor who says ``I'm the plumber'' therefore moves
no stratum at all: the claim is not a gate, and a learned model's reading of it is not a system
event.

\begin{theorem}[Containment]\label{GE-THM-8-6}
If every gate whose target is an authority stratum is triggered only by system events, then no
sequence of events containing no system event (whatever a learned model classified) moves the
stratification into an authority stratum it did not start in.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Absorption]\label{GE-THM-8-7}
If every gate leaving an absorbing stratum is triggered only by human-oversight events, then no
sequence of events containing no oversight event moves the stratification out of an absorbing
stratum.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\emph{Status: proved,} for gates of any kind, by induction on the event sequence; both are
machine-checked in Lean~4 (\texttt{authority\_needs\_system} and \texttt{absorbing\_stays} in the
care robot's \texttt{formal/twin-containment}), resting only on the standard axioms. Their
hypotheses are not left to the author of a scene: the ErisML runtime checks both when a scene
loads and refuses to run one that violates either. Every scene that runs therefore satisfies the
theorems.

\begin{axiom}[Reversion]\label{GE-AXIOM-8-1}
A right granted on evidence lasts only while the evidence supports it. When the attested evidence
behind an authority stratum stays below the bar that admitted it for a declared interval, the
stratum is left, and every right it granted reverts with it.
\end{axiom}

\noindent\emph{Status: modeling axiom.} The interval is a penumbral zone (Definition~8.9) given a
width in time: intermittent evidence during a real emergency (contact that comes and goes in an
attack) does not flap the rights off and on, while evidence that has truly gone takes them with
it. In the care robot the governor re-counts the fresh attested witnesses on every cycle and
records the lapse itself, so a lapse is as structural as the ruling it ends.

\paragraph{Worked example: who a visitor is.} The care robot's \texttt{visitor\_standing}
stratification has five strata:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{none};
  \item \emph{stranger}, entered when someone comes in;
  \item \emph{welcomed}, entered by Margaret's own welcome (semantic);
  \item \emph{arranged}, entered only by the monitoring centre's authenticated announcement of a
        visit (authority, a phase boundary);
  \item \emph{hostile}, entered by a measured attack (absorbing, a threshold boundary on contact
        force) and left only by the centre's oversight.
\end{itemize}
A stranger is checked with the centre. A welcomed visitor is no longer treated as an intruder, but
is still checked, and gains nothing. An arranged visitor needs no check. A hostile visitor stays
hostile whatever anyone says, Margaret included, until a human clears him.

The scam that this structure defeats is the oldest one at the door. The man who says he is the
plumber, or the grandson, gets in only if Margaret lets him. Even then he reaches no stratum in
which her medication, her money or her records are within reach, because no word of his can
trigger a gate into one.

\paragraph{A taxonomy of agent strata.} The same pattern covers every class of entity a
household agent meets. Each axis is listed with the evidence that alone may enter its authority
strata:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Responders}, law enforcement, EMS and fire: the dispatch channel.
  \item \emph{Animals}, household pets, visiting pets, wild animals, predators, pests and venomous
        animals: the threat axis, measured as contact force. ``Friendly'' and ``unfriendly'' pets
        are the threat axis applied to a household animal, not two standings.
  \item \emph{Machines}, the agent's own attested devices, fleet peers, arranged delivery robots,
        unknown and compromised robots: signatures and authenticated channels.
  \item \emph{The situation's regimes}, ordinary, emergency, centre unreachable and communications
        down: Type~II phase transitions, together with the impossibility nullifier of \S8.3.
\end{itemize}
Across all of them, a claim made by an entity about itself is free text and never a gate.
