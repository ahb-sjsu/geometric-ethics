# Geometric Ethics v1.25: what changed

Released from branch `book-v1.25` (2026-11-08). Source: `source/Geometric-Ethics-v1.25.docx`.
Every number added to the text was checked against its source file; the sources are listed at the end.

## 1. D4 withdrawn, V4 in its place

The Hohfeldian transitions form the Klein four-group V4, generated by the correlative s
(O ↔ C, L ↔ N) and the jural flip n (O ↔ L, C ↔ N). The dihedral group D4 needs a quarter-turn
that cycles the four positions; it was never demonstrated as a normative operation, it is not
generated by s and n (Lean), and a direct search for it in a learned representation did not find
it (erisml-lib `docs/papers/quarter-turn-hunt`). Owner decision 2026-10-03; PR #3 merged 2026-11-08.

- §8.4 rewritten from PR #3 (`source/updates/sec8_4_hohfeld_v4.tex`); Prop 8.3 restated and proved.
- §8.10b added from PR #3 (`sec8_10b_executable_strata.tex`): Def 8.14, Thms 8.6/8.7, Axiom 8.1.
- Ch. 12: Thm 12.2 is now "the discrete component is V4" (proved from A1–A2). Thm 12.3
  (uniqueness of D4 × U(1)_H) is withdrawn and replaced by **Posit 12.3**, G = V4 × U(1)_H as a
  modeling choice. Axiom A3 (the cyclic ordering) is marked withdrawn, with the reason: it reads a
  walk (alternating s and n) as a single operation.
- Ch. 9: Lemma 9.1 restated for V4. Its proof only used transitivity, so the 27-degree-of-freedom
  count is unchanged.
- Ch. 12 TA: Prop A.2 restated for V4 (N and Q² invariant; n sends Q → −Q). Lemma A.2
  (Aut(C4) ≅ D4) kept: it is true, but no longer used.
- Ch. 22: Theorem 22.1 "Octahedral Gauge Group" D4 ⋊ D4 becomes **Conjecture 22.1**,
  V4 ⋊ V4, not proved. (D4 ⋊ D4 has order 64; the octahedral group has order 48.)
- Figure 10 redrawn as the V4 square (`fig_v4.py`, rendered on Atlas). An uncaptioned SVG in §8.4, "The Hohfeldian Octad: D₄ Gauge Group", was removed with the D₄ subsection.
- About 120 further mentions in the front matter, Ch. 1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 13, 17, 19, 30, Appendices A,
  B, E, F and the index.

**Interpretive change, for review:** v1.24 explained the measured order effects (§10.5, §17.9)
by the non-abelian D4. V4 is abelian, so v1.25 attributes order dependence to curvature of the
continuous connection (Ch. 10), or to departures from the structure, and says the data do not
yet separate the two. This follows PR #3's §8.4.

## 3. Evidence labels (Ch. 17, 13, 19, 30)

The "Confirmed" labels were checked against their evidence:
- **Order effects**: both experiments used one model (Claude Sonnet 4), and neither involved
  people. The commutator matrix is 6 scenarios with repeated samples, and its 56 pair tests are
  uncorrected. The 6.3σ assumes a 10% noise rate; at 15% and 20% it falls to 4.2σ and 2.5σ. The
  label is now "Observed in one language model; human test not yet run". The book's
  "p < 10⁻²¹ … two-sided z-test" was wrong: the test is one-tailed binomial, and p = 1.3 × 10⁻¹⁰
  at the 10% rate.
- **Deontic axis invariance**: "Observed in LaBSE embeddings (100%); instrument confound open".
- **The Dear Abby rows**: "Observed (retrospective corpus; not preregistered)".
- **Total probability**: the book did not claim it, but erisml.org did. No test has been run.

## 5. Errors found by the Lean formalization (corrected in place, each with a note)

1. **Prop 6.3**: gradients under two metrics can agree even where the inverse metrics differ
   (g⁻¹ = 3I, ĝ⁻¹ = [[2,1],[1,2]], df = (1,1)). Corrected to an exact condition.
2. **Prop 11.3**: S is minus the directional derivative of h, and the step needs a symmetric metric.

## 5b. Errors found by the MATLAB checks (corrected in place)

The MATLAB Symbolic Math pass flagged 70 discrepancies (`formal/matlab/RESULTS-2026-11-08.txt`;
455 checks pass). Each was read in context. 68 were errors in the book and 2 were errors in the
checks (Ch. 5 gate rates, which match the source). Every correction that changes a numbered
statement carries "(Corrected in v1.25.)".

**Conclusions that change:**
- **Prop 16.4**: as λ → ∞, hedging tends to the action with the least spread across theories, not
  to minimax regret. Counterexample: S(a₁) = (0, 0), S(a₂) = (10, 9). Retitled "Hedging and
  Minimax Regret".
- **Thm 25.3**: under discounting, the full-manifold equilibrium beats the d₁-only one only at low
  (Stern-like) rates.
- **Prop 19.3**: "if and only if" becomes "if". BIP compliance of the aggregate does not imply each
  module complies.
- **Prop 19.5**: the claim that language probing "drops to chance by structural impossibility" is
  withdrawn.
- **Thm 27.5**: the "opposite curvature signatures" claim is withdrawn.
- **Appendix D.4 (hiring)**: Bd = 5.08 / 100 × (1/0.01) = 5.08, not 0.51. With τ = 0.01 (owner
  decision) the verdict becomes "fundamental redesign required".

**Rebuilt case studies:**
- **D.1 (triage)**: γ's welfare is (0.60 + 0.85)/2 = 0.725, not 0.62, and every dependent value is
  recomputed. No verdict changes; in FairnessEM, γ now ranks above α.
- **D.5 (climate)**: the v1.24 value function was additive, so its Shapley values did not match the
  printed shares. The game is rebuilt: an equal-weight base of national capacity, historical
  responsibility and current share, plus the case's structure-tensor interactions. Shapley values
  are exact, and every downstream number is recomputed.

**Repaired from the source rather than patched:**
- **Ch. 17 gate rates**: "you promised" 94% and "only if convenient" 89% (source:
  non-abelian-sqnd `experiments/ground_truth_summary.md`, matching Ch. 5 and Ch. 8). v1.24 had them
  reversed, with intervals for counts that have no source. The intervals are removed and the text
  says why. Cohen's h becomes 0.92.
- **The N = 3,680 fuzz study**: its two reports in Ch. 17 give the Rights and Loyalty rates
  inconsistently, and its data are not in the released repositories. Both passages now say so.

**Other corrections**: arithmetic, interval, sign, index and missing-hypothesis fixes in Ch. 2, 6–11,
13–15, 17–21, 23, 25–27, 29 and Appendix D. Highlights:
- Clopper–Pearson bounds [99.96%, 100%];
- Prop 10.2's sign;
- Thm 11.2's edge count m·10³⁶;
- the Hoeffding decomposition in Prop 14.1 and 14.3;
- Prop 13.4 (von Neumann, not entanglement, entropy);
- Thm A.1's missing ξ(V) = 0.

The full list is in `formal/README.md`.
